Jin Xie (谢觐)

Associate Professor with Tenure
Peking University HSBC Business School

I am a tenured Associate Professor at Peking University HSBC Business School. I joined PHBS as an Assistant Professor in 2022. Previously, I was an Assistant Professor of Accounting at the Chinese University of Hong Kong. I received my Ph.D. in Accounting from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2014.

My research interests include the intersection between accounting and macroeconomics, corporate fraud, accounting standards, antitrust, and the Chinese economy.
